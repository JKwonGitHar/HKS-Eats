Paying: Harvard Dining says all its cafés accept cash, Mastercard, Visa, American Express, and BoardPlus. Several also list the GradPlus declining-balance plan for graduate and professional students, tagged above.

Closures: Most cafés close for Harvard's winter break, academic breaks, and summer; student-run spots (Gato Rojo, Lamont) also close for reading and exam periods. Hours are the academic-year schedule and can change, so check the café's page before a special trip.

Local restaurants: Included if they had a Google Maps rating of 4.0 stars or higher and at least 100 reviews when checked on September 24, 2026. The review minimum screens out new places with only a handful of ratings. Ratings, prices and hours change, so tap a restaurant to check Google Maps before you go. Their walking times are estimates.

Source: Harvard University Dining Services, Graduate & Professional Dining, pulled September 2026. Walking times are estimates from 79 JFK Street and are not from Harvard Dining or Google. This is an unofficial student guide.
